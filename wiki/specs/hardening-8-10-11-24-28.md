## Goal
A visitor or tester never waits on a dead request, never sees a false message, and an operator can tell from our own log what happened to each call.

## Context
- #23 (2026-11-08): replies took 100 to 200 s, the backend timed out at 120 s and showed a false "no model connected" text. Cloudflare closes a tunnel request at about 100 s.
- #10: Ollama unloads the model after 5 minutes idle; a cold load takes up to about 70 s.
- #8: a 40 MB image and a 5 MB question were accepted and reached the model step.
- #28: 8 `except Exception` blocks hide failures; the log has no duration or outcome.

## Not in scope
- Per-visitor GPU limit (#9): Cloudflare rate-limit rule, Juan's dashboard.
- Streaming, per-kiosk prompt, moving to `/v1`.

## Approach
1. **Size limits.** A middleware on `POST /api/*` and `POST /v1/*`: no `Content-Length` gives 411; over `MAX_BODY_MB` (default 8) gives 413. The check runs before the body is read. A question over 4,000 characters gives 413.
2. **Warm model.** Every kiosk call sends `"keep_alive": -1`. On startup the backend asks Ollama to load the model (`/api/generate`, no prompt, `keep_alive: -1`), so the first visitor after a restart is warm. Failure is only logged. Ollama still evicts the model if a team caller asks for another one.
3. **Answer before Cloudflare gives up.** `OLLAMA_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` default 120 becomes 90.
4. **Honest outcomes.** `_call_ollama` returns an outcome: `ok`, `timeout`, `unreachable`, `error`, `empty`. Unreachable, error and empty keep today's stub reply (`stub: true`). A **timeout** gets HTTP 504 with a different text ("The agent is taking longer than usual. Please try again in a moment.", ES/EN), `stub: false`, `timeout: true`.
5. **One log line per model call:** `call route=<chat|vision|v1> user=<name> outcome=<...> ms=<n> model=<name>`. Errors also log `model_error reason=<ExceptionClassName>`. Refused calls log `refused path=<path> why=<missing|bad_key|app_key_on_v1>`. **Never logged on kiosk calls:** visitor IP, question, answer, image. The key value is never logged.
6. **`/v1` body not an object** gives 400, not 500 (#11).

## Tests (each fails on today's code)
- Body over the limit gives 413 on `/api/chat`, `/api/vision`, `/v1/chat/completions`, and no HTTP client toward Ollama is opened; a chunked body gives 411; a question over 4,000 characters gives 413.
- A fake `httpx` `ReadTimeout` gives 504, `timeout: true`, `stub: false`, and the timeout text. A `ConnectError` gives 200, `stub: true` (the honest fallback; part of #12). A full queue (`_model_sem` with 0 free, short wait) gives 503.
- The payload sent to Ollama has `keep_alive: -1`; startup (TestClient as a context manager) sends one warm-up request.
- Each outcome prints one `call …` line with the expected fields; the line never contains the question text.
- `/v1/chat/completions` with a JSON list and a valid key gives 400.

## How we check it live
1. From outside, `POST /api/chat` with a 9 MB body: 413 on `kiosk.ciaocorp.com`, the team hostname and the tailnet.
2. Restart the service, wait 60 s, `ollama ps` shows the model loaded; the first real question answers in under 30 s.
3. Ask a real question in the kiosk: the journal shows `call route=chat user=app:intercambio outcome=ok ms=…`, and no question text.
4. Wait 10 minutes idle; `ollama ps` still shows the model.

## Deploy and rollback
Pull and restart. Rollback: revert and restart. **Risk:** a permanently loaded model keeps about 4 to 5 GB of the 7.4 GB; a second model requested through `/v1` makes Ollama swap. This was already the state during every test today.

## Risks and open questions
- `MAX_BODY_MB` 8 should fit a phone photo sent through the API; the page downsizes to 1024 px, so the page never comes near it.
- A visitor who gets "taking longer than usual" will retry and queue another call. Acceptable for now.
