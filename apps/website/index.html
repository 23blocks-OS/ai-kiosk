Three kiosks, one box

Intercambio

For Spanish-speaking newcomers at a community center. The agent reads a form or letter under the camera, explains it, translates it, and helps with the next step. Building first.

Shop Operator

For small shops running legacy Windows software. The agent looks at the shop's screen and operates it — appointments, records, the work the software was already built for.

How it works

  1. You talk to the agent — tap the mic or type, in Spanish or English.
  2. The agent looks — the small window shows exactly what it sees: your document, or the shop's screen.
  3. It answers here — in the room, on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The agent is one vision-language model (Qwen3-VL 4B) running on a Jetson Orin Nano in the box, with local speech recognition and speech synthesis. Details in docs/architecture.md.

Private by construction

Nothing is recorded. Nothing leaves the device. The camera starts only when you tap to enable it, and the Picture-in-Picture window lets you see — and hide — what the agent sees at any moment.
